# Satellite embeddings 2017 (area- and population-weighted)

> Generated by `geo4bolivia build` from [`datapackage.json`](datapackage.json). Edit that file, not this one.

## Overview

Sixty-four satellite embedding dimensions for Bolivia's 339 municipalities of the 2015 map in 2017, in two versions: the area-weighted mean (A00–A63) and the population-weighted mean (A00_pw–A63_pw), over each municipality, of the annual 10 m embeddings from the Google Satellite Embedding dataset (AlphaEarth Foundations), which summarise Sentinel-2, Landsat and other imagery. The area-weighted version describes the whole territory; the population-weighted one, the places where people live. The dimensions are abstract features, designed as inputs to machine-learning models, e.g. to predict poverty.

- **Dataset id:** `emb`
- **Categories:** Environment, Economy

## Notes

- The values belong to DS4Bolivia's polygons of the 2015 map, so they are published on that map, map339 (339 municipalities; boundaries in geo/boundaries/map339). Its municipalities keep their geo IDs in the 343-unit map of 2025 (map343), except that six of them (Colquechaca, Mizque, Porco, Tomave, San Ignacio and Santa Ana) have since lost territory to four new units (San Pedro de Macha, TIOC-Raqaypampa, TIOC-Jatun Ayllu Yura and TIOC-Territorio Indígena Multiétnico), which have no values here. geo/crosswalks/map339__map343.csv gives the area shares to move the values onto map343 (e.g. with geo.overlay).
- The dimensions are not interpretable one by one and should not be aggregated; their aggregation rule is `none`.
- Computed by DS4Bolivia in Google Earth Engine on its own municipal polygons (`asdf_id`), geoBoundaries v4 polygons the 2015 map published as map339 (geo/boundaries/map339), which differs slightly from the SDSN Bolivia 2025 map (map343).
- A00–A63 are area-weighted: every 10 m pixel counts the same (code/aggregate-satellite-embedings-to-adm.js in DS4Bolivia). A00_pw–A63_pw are population-weighted: each pixel is weighted by WorldPop 100 m population, spread evenly over its 10 m pixels (code/aggregate-satellite-embedings-to-adm-pop-weighted1.js in DS4Bolivia), so they describe where people live. Until version 0.9.0 the population-weighted version was a dataset of its own, `embpw`, with the same column names A00–A63.
