## Overview

The Sustainable Development Index (IDS) of the Atlas de los ODS en Bolivia 2026 (SDSN Bolivia), which summarises 81 indicators of 15 Sustainable Development Goals, and its national ranking, for Bolivia's 343 municipalities and indigenous autonomies (GAIOC), near the 2012 census and near the 2024 census (stored as 2012 and 2024), with the census population of both years and the 2024 urbanization rate as context.

- **Dataset id:** `sdg`
- **Categories:** Social, Economy, Environment, Institutions

## Scale: municipality

- **Units:** 343 of 343 municipality units
- **Period:** 2012–2024, years 2012, 2024 (686 rows: one per unit and year)
- **Source geometry:** Municipal statistics of the Atlas, linked by INE municipality code

## Notes

- Both periods are stored at nominal years: 2012 for the Atlas's initial values (~2012) and 2024 for its final values (~2024). Each indicator's actual reference years differ (e.g. 2008 and 2023 for the health survey, three-year averages for volatile indicators) and are given in its description and in `geo:reference_years`.
- Load one variable in both periods, e.g. `geo.load("sdg", variables="index_sdg")`, and its change with `df.pivot(index="geo_id", columns="year", values="index_sdg")`.
- The units are the 343 municipalities and indigenous autonomies (GAIOC) of 2025. Four of them were created after the 2012 census (TIOC-Raqaypampa, San Pedro de Macha, TIOC-Jatun Ayllu Yura and TIOC-Territorio Indígena Multiétnico); the Atlas gives them values in both periods.
- The IDS and the ranking are not additive (`none`); `population` sums, and `urbanization` aggregates as a population-weighted mean (`geo.aggregate(geo.load("sdg"), to="department", dataset="sdg")`).
- The goal indices and the 81 indicators are in the goal datasets `sdg01` ... `sdg17` (no 12 or 14).
