## Overview

The SDG 11 (Sustainable cities and communities) index of the Atlas de los ODS en Bolivia 2026 (SDSN Bolivia) and the 7 indicators behind it, for Bolivia's 343 municipalities and indigenous autonomies (GAIOC), at two points in time: near the 2012 census and near the 2024 census, stored as 2012 and 2024 so that their change can be studied.

- **Dataset id:** `sdg11`
- **Categories:** Social

## Notes

- Both periods are stored at nominal years: 2012 for the Atlas's initial values (~2012) and 2024 for its final values (~2024). Each indicator's actual reference years differ (e.g. 2008 and 2023 for the health survey, three-year averages for volatile indicators) and are given in its description and in `geo:reference_years`.
- Load one variable in both periods, e.g. `geo.load("sdg11", variables="index_sdg11", names=True)`, and its change per unit with `geo.change(df, "index_sdg11")`.
- The units are the 343 municipalities and indigenous autonomies (GAIOC) of 2025. Four of them were created after the 2012 census (TIOC-Raqaypampa, San Pedro de Macha, TIOC-Jatun Ayllu Yura and TIOC-Territorio Indígena Multiétnico); the Atlas gives them values in both periods.
- Aggregating to provinces, departments or Bolivia (`geo.aggregate`, `geo.combine`) averages the municipal values weighted by census population (`weighted_mean:population`; the 2012 census for ~2012 and the 2024 census for ~2024, loaded from the `sdg` dataset through `geo:weights`): e.g. `geo.aggregate(geo.load("sdg11"), to="department", dataset="sdg11")`. These are population-weighted approximations, not the Atlas's own department figures.
