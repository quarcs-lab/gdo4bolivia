## Overview

The SDG 7 (Affordable and clean energy) index of the Atlas de los ODS en Bolivia 2026 (SDSN Bolivia) and the 4 indicators behind it, for Bolivia's 343 municipalities and indigenous autonomies (GAIOC), at two points in time: near the 2012 census and near the 2024 census, stored as 2012 and 2024 so that their change can be studied.

- **Dataset id:** `sdg07`
- **Categories:** Economy, Environment

## Scale: municipality

- **Units:** 343 of 343 municipality units
- **Period:** 2012–2024, years 2012, 2024 (686 rows: one per unit and year)
- **Source geometry:** Municipal statistics of the Atlas, linked by INE municipality code

## Notes

- Both periods are stored at nominal years: 2012 for the Atlas's initial values (~2012) and 2024 for its final values (~2024). Each indicator's actual reference years differ (e.g. 2008 and 2023 for the health survey, three-year averages for volatile indicators) and are given in its description and in `geo:reference_years`.
- Load one variable in both periods, e.g. `geo.load("sdg07", variables="index_sdg7", names=True)`, and its change per unit with `geo.change(df, "index_sdg7")`.
- The units are the 343 municipalities and indigenous autonomies (GAIOC) of 2025. Four of them were created after the 2012 census (TIOC-Raqaypampa, San Pedro de Macha, TIOC-Jatun Ayllu Yura and TIOC-Territorio Indígena Multiétnico); the Atlas gives them values in both periods.
- Aggregating to provinces, departments or Bolivia (`geo.aggregate`, `geo.combine`) averages the municipal values weighted by census population (`weighted_mean:population`; the 2012 census for ~2012 and the 2024 census for ~2024, loaded from the `sdg` dataset through `geo:weights`): e.g. `geo.aggregate(geo.load("sdg07"), to="department", dataset="sdg07")`. These are population-weighted approximations, not the Atlas's own department figures.
