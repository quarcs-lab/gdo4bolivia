## Overview

The SDG 10 (Reduced inequalities) index of the Atlas de los ODS en Bolivia 2026 (SDSN Bolivia) and the 6 indicators behind it, for Bolivia's 343 municipalities and indigenous autonomies (GAIOC), at two points in time: near the 2012 census and near the 2024 census, stored as 2012 and 2024 so that their change can be studied.

- **Dataset id:** `sdg10`
- **Categories:** Social

## Scale: municipality

- **Units:** 343 of 343 municipality units
- **Period:** 2012–2024, years 2012, 2024 (686 rows: one per unit and year)
- **Source geometry:** Municipal statistics of the Atlas, linked by INE municipality code

## Notes

- Both periods are stored at nominal years: 2012 for the Atlas's initial values (~2012) and 2024 for its final values (~2024). Each indicator's actual reference years differ (e.g. 2008 and 2023 for the health survey, three-year averages for volatile indicators) and are given in its description and in `geo:reference_years`.
- Load one variable in both periods, e.g. `geo.load("sdg10", variables="index_sdg10")`, and its change with `df.pivot(index="geo_id", columns="year", values="index_sdg10")`.
- The units are the 343 municipalities and indigenous autonomies (GAIOC) of 2025. Four of them were created after the 2012 census (TIOC-Raqaypampa, San Pedro de Macha, TIOC-Jatun Ayllu Yura and TIOC-Territorio Indígena Multiétnico); the Atlas gives them values in both periods.
- The indicators are rates, shares, ratios or indices, so their aggregation rule is `none`. Population-weighted averages can use the census population of the `sdg` dataset: `geo.aggregate(df, to="department", rules={"index_sdg10": "weighted_mean:population"}, weights=geo.load("sdg", variables="population"))`.
